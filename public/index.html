Spring 2

A letter from Grandma Hazel

Dear Rosa,

The farm is yours now, and it will teach you as you go. Follow the path east past the signpost into Hollow Square, where Pip and Poppy's General Store sells seeds. Hoe a patch of grass inside the fence, plant your seeds, and water them every day. When your can runs dry, the pond fills it again.

Sleep in the farmhouse bed when the day is done. Crops grow while you rest. When a crop is ripe and full, pick it with your hand and put it in the wooden box by the house. It ships in the night and the coins are yours by morning.

Be kind to the neighbours who stop by. They were fond of this place long before us.

All my love, Grandma Hazel
